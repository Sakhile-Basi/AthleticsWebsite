Welcome to Team SA

Team SA, in partnership with the Department of Sports, Arts, and Culture, is committed to elevating the profile of athletics. Track and field athletes dedicate themselves tirelessly, pushing their bodies to the limit in training to realize their full potential. Their pursuit extends beyond personal achievement; they strive to transcend the boundaries we often impose upon ourselves, showcasing the remarkable capabilities of the human body.


Despite their extraordinary talents in sprinting, jumping, and throwing, track and field athletes often find themselves overlooked and underappreciated. At Team SA, we are determined to change this narrative by demystifying the sport and fostering greater understanding and support among the public.


Our initiative launches at a pivotal moment, coinciding with the upcoming Olympic Games, the premier platform for track and field competition and a spectacle enjoyed by audiences worldwide. By capitalizing on this global stage, we aim to amplify our efforts in promoting the sport, attracting increased interest, endorsements, and resources. With enhanced support and investment, we believe our nation harbors the potential to emerge as a dominant force in athletics.


Together, we can nurture and empower our exceptional talent, ensuring they receive the support they need to thrive and excel on the international stage.
